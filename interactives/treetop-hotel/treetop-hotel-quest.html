Learning Intention & Success Criteria ▼

Learning Intention

We are learning to solve connected, multi-step problems by choosing the right maths for each step.

Success Criteria

  • I can work out what a question is asking and plan my steps before I calculate.
  • I can use the order of operations, fractions, percentages and equations to find unknown amounts.
  • I can find perimeters, areas and volumes of shapes made from simpler shapes.
  • I can check that each answer makes sense in the story and carry it accurately into the next problem.

A maths quest in seven parts

The Treetop Hotel

Inherit a forest. Build a hotel in the trees without cutting a single one down. Then make it pay its way.

Begin Chapter 1

Your great-aunt Marigold was a botanist who spent sixty years caring for Whisperwood, a patch of old forest in the hills. She has left it to you, with her savings and one condition: build a hotel in the treetops without cutting down a single tree, and make it pay its way.

Every answer you find is needed later, so the questions form a chain. Get one wrong and the next ones go wrong too, so check as you go.

Choose your floor

Enter the hotel on whichever floor you're comfortable with. It doesn't matter which floor you enter on, so long as you come in and take part. It's where you finish that matters.

Every floor tells the same story and reaches the same checkpoint numbers, so you can change floor at the start of any chapter. Your answers are saved on this device.

How the quest works

Work in your maths book

Write the question number, your working and your answer. Then type your answer into the page to check it.

Keep the Hotel Ledger

Copy the ledger below into your book. Whenever you see Ledger, update it.

Stop at every Checkpoint

Check your ledger numbers on the page, then show your teacher before the next chapter.

Stuck? Ask the page

Press "I'm not sure how to start" or "Give me the formula". After three wrong tries, a hint appears.

Canopy Challenges are extra

The hardest questions in the quest. Try them once you have finished the chapter.
