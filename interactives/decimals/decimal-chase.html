Decimal Chase

Pick a decimal. Then chase down one that is higher or lower, before time runs out.

Learning Intention & Success Criteria ▼

Learning Intention

We are learning to compare decimals that are very close in value by using place value.

Success Criteria

  • I can read decimals with up to five decimal places.
  • I can decide which of two close decimals is higher or lower.
  • I can find a decimal that is higher or lower than a target, even under time pressure.
  • I can explain my comparison using place value columns.

How to play

1

Decimals float around the screen. They are all very close in value, so look carefully.

2

Tap any decimal to pick it. It turns gold.

3

The game then tells you to tap a decimal that is higher or lower than your pick. Find one before the timer runs out.

4

A wrong tap costs a life. You have 3 lives and 5 rounds. Good luck.
